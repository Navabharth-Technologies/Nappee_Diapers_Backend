Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 31, 2026

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nappee Diapers ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use our mobile application, WhatsApp ordering service, and associated delivery platforms.

1. Information We Collect

To provide fast delivery of diapers and sanitary hygiene products, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact Information: Customer name, phone number, and delivery address provided during order placement or WhatsApp chat.
  • Location Data: Approximate and precise GPS location coordinates shared by you to facilitate accurate address verification and fast doorstep delivery by our delivery partners. Location is only collected when you choose to share your delivery address or track an active delivery.
  • Order & Transaction Records: Details of products ordered, quantity, order timestamp, order status, total price, and payment reference numbers.
  • Payment Details: All payments are securely processed through our certified third-party payment partner, Razorpay. We do not collect or store credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for genuine business operations:

  • To process, confirm, and fulfill your orders.
  • To coordinate with delivery partners to navigate and deliver packages to your specified location.
  • To send automated order confirmations, payment receipts, and delivery updates via WhatsApp and SMS.
  • To provide prompt customer support and resolve any delivery inquiries.
  • To prevent fraudulent transactions and maintain platform security.

3. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We respect your privacy and do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for marketing or advertising purposes. Information is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with:

  • Assigned Delivery Partners: Only your name, delivery address/location, contact phone number, and package details are shared with the driver assigned to deliver your specific order.
  • Payment Processing: Razorpay Software Private Limited for secure processing of online payments (UPI, cards, net banking).
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Microsoft Azure cloud services for secure encrypted database storage and API hosting.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process in India.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures, including HTTPS encryption in transit and restricted database access controls, to safeguard your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion Requests

We retain order history and transaction records as necessary to satisfy legal, tax, accounting, and warranty requirements. If you wish to request the deletion of your customer information or order records, you can contact our support team at any time by emailing support@nappeediapers.com or messaging our official WhatsApp support line with your registered phone number. We will process your deletion request within 15 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

While our products include infant diapers and hygiene essentials, our application and ordering service are intended strictly for adults and parents aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
